Make the app sound like you

Many children find comfort in hearing a parent’s voice, so we thought we’d add the ability for parents to do voiceovers for navigation and exploration around the app, allowing young people to have a sense of familiarity in what might otherwise be a new world.

You can add your voice to everything your child explores in two ways:

Set up your voice: make one short recording, about a minute, and we’ll use ElevenLabs to create voiceovers in your voice for every place, including new ones.

Record manually: record each one yourself in Grown-ups, at the top of the screen.

For the best voice, use your phone

It looks like you’re on a laptop or computer. A phone’s microphone gives a much more natural voice. You can still carry on here if you prefer.

Before you begin
  • Find a quiet room and turn off the TV or music
  • Record on your phone. It gives a much more natural voice than a laptop
  • Hold your phone a couple of inches from your mouth
  • Read the way you’d read a story to your child: calm and warm
  • Keep the same pace all the way through. If you slip, just carry on
  • Scroll gently with one finger as you read

Before we create your voice

We’ll send your recording to ElevenLabs, a company that makes AI voices. They’ll create a copy of your voice that we only use to read this app’s words to your child.

Your voice and voiceovers are private to your family’s account. No other families can access them.

You can delete your voice at any time in Grown-ups.
